## 1. Why

Until now every case and every request came from the synthetic seed. Once
an entity and its people are onboarded (WO-012), nothing in HIVE let
Honeybee begin work: the first case on production could not exist, and
the client could not be asked for anything. The brief's product is "one
calm view of status, evidence, questions, requests, ownership, and next
action"; intake is where that view starts.

## 2. Outcome

Four in-app actions for staff at AAL2, each a reviewed server transition
behind a confirmation, and one new trail entry:

| Who                    | Where                        | What                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| intake                 | Home → "Open a new case"     | opens a case as a **draft** with a title; the client sees it as "Being set up"; nothing is asked of them yet                                           |
| intake                 | the case                     | **records the intake**: the case shows as received to everyone, and the trail gains "Received by Honeybee"; or **discards** a draft that holds nothing |
| intake or the preparer | the case → "Ask the client…" | **opens a request** with a title, an optional detail, a due date of none, 7, 14 or 30 days, and optionally one checked document it is about            |
| intake or the preparer | the case, on a request's row | **closes** an open or answered request; the case stays where it is                                                                                     |

The first request moves a received case to waiting on documents; further
requests leave the case as it is. Home derives its guidance for a case
nobody authored guidance for: what waits on the client is counted from
the open requests, and who holds the next move follows the status.

Behind them, migration `20260930120016`: `open_case`,
`record_case_intake`, `open_request`, `close_request`, and
`discard_case_draft`, security definer, for the staff of the exact scope
at AAL2, each idempotent by key, version-checked, receipted with ids and
facts only, with the bounds in `app_private.intake_limits()`.

## 3. Requirements

- R1. **Only intake opens, records, or discards a case; intake or the
  preparer asks and closes.** The server decides from membership in the
  exact scope at AAL2; the app only offers what the role may do.
- R2. **Nothing is asked on a draft.** A request needs a received case or
  one gathering evidence; the client sees a draft as "Being set up".
- R3. **Every write carries the key, the version, and the exact scope**;
  a stale version is refused (`case_changed`, `request_changed`), a
  repeated key returns the first result and writes nothing.
- R4. **Text is cleaned and bounded.** A title is 2–120 printable
  characters on one line, trimmed and single-spaced; a detail is up to
  2,000 characters, empty allowed, line breaks allowed, no other control
  character; the app bounds the same way before the round trip, and the
  server is the authority.
- R5. **A due date is one to 365 days out, or none**; the app offers
  none, 7, 14 and 30.
- R6. **A request's subject is a checked document of THIS case**, or
  nothing; a document of another case, of another scope, or not checked
  is refused (`document_not_checked`).
- R7. **Bounds per workspace and per case**: 20 drafts per workspace,
  50 open requests per case.
- R8. **Receipts and trail entries carry ids, statuses, versions, roles
  and facts; never a title or a detail.**
- R9. **A recorded case cannot be discarded; a draft that holds anything
  cannot be discarded** (`case_not_draft`, `case_has_children`).

## 4. Threats and how they are met

| Threat                                                                     | Met by                                                                                                                                           |

| T1. A client, or staff at AAL1, or staff of another entity, authors a case | R1; pgTAP 016; the harness (eleven 403 negatives across the five functions with a client, an AAL1, and a foreign-scope token)                    |
| T2. A request is opened on a case the client has not been told about       | R2; pgTAP 016; the harness (`case_not_open_for_requests` on the draft)                                                                           |
| T3. A lost answer writes twice, or a stale screen overwrites               | R3; pgTAP 016; the harness (every creation and transition replayed by key; every wrong version refused)                                          |
| T4. Words leak into receipts, logs, or the trail                           | R8; the harness scans the receipts for the title and the detail; the trail carries enumerated kinds                                              |
| T5. A request points at a document of another case                         | R6; the harness (a document checked on A1's case, and a random id, both refused for the A2 case)                                                 |
| T6. Runaway authoring fills a workspace                                    | R7; the harness opens drafts until `too_many_drafts` at exactly 20, then discards them all                                                       |
| T7. A draft with evidence disappears                                       | R9; pgTAP 016 (`case_has_children`)                                                                                                              |
| T8. The app offers an action the server refuses                            | The rules are pure and tested (`review-rules`, `intake-rules`); the server refusal is worded on the screen and a stale one sends the person back |

## 5. Decisions recorded (ratified by Kody, 2026-09-30)

| Decision                                    | As built                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A case begins as a draft the client can see | shown as "Being set up"; no trail entry until the intake is recorded, which writes "Received by Honeybee"                                                                  |
| The first request moves the case            | INTAKE_RECORDED → EVIDENCE_PENDING on the first request; later requests leave the case as it is; closing a request moves nothing                                           |
| Due dates                                   | none, 7, 14 or 30 days in the app; 1–365 on the server                                                                                                                     |
| Bounds                                      | 20 drafts per workspace, 50 open requests per case, 120-character titles, 2,000-character details                                                                          |
| Discarding a draft deletes the row          | only a childless draft; the receipt of the opening and of the discard stay                                                                                                 |
| Derived guidance on Home                    | for a case with no authored attention item or next action: the open requests are counted for the client, and the next move's owner follows the status (wording for Stacie) |
| The device lane signs in any staff identity | the enrollment runner takes `--as <synthetic staff email>` and `--scope "<chooser row>"`; the shared MFA flows take the chooser on the runner's word, never optionally     |
| Migrations 015 and 016 on production        | pushed together 2026-09-30, before any first use, as WO-012 recorded; production holds no rows                                                                             |

  device; the staging project has the migration and the pgTAP proof.
