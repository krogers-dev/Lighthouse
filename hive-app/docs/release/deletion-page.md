## Deleting your HIVE account

HIVE is an app for Honeybee Accounting's clients and staff. Your access
is set up by your Honeybee team; you cannot create an account yourself,
and you can ask for your account to be deleted at any time.

### How to ask

1. Open HIVE and sign in.
2. Go to **Account**.
3. Under **Delete your account**, choose **Request account deletion** and
   confirm.

You can withdraw the request from the same place until Honeybee
completes it. If you can no longer sign in, email the support address
shown in the app and ask for the deletion.

### What deletion removes

- Your access to HIVE.
- The account you sign in with (your email address and sign-in
  identity).

### What deletion does not remove

Records that Honeybee Accounting keeps for the business you work with,
including documents and answers you provided through HIVE, are kept
under Honeybee's record-keeping policy and are not deleted by this
request. Where those records name who acted, they keep an internal
reference and not your name or email once the account is gone.

### What happens next

Honeybee completes the deletion and confirms it by email.
