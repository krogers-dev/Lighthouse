## HIVE Privacy Policy

**Effective date: [date of publication]**

HIVE is a mobile app made by Honeybee Accounting for its clients and
staff. This policy explains what information HIVE handles, why, who else
sees it, how long it is kept, and what you can ask us to do. It is written
to be read, not skimmed; if anything in it is unclear, write to us at
**info@myhbcfo.com**.

### 1. Who is responsible

Honeybee Accounting, [legal entity name and form], [postal address],
operates HIVE and is responsible for the information described here.
Contact: info@myhbcfo.com.

### 2. What HIVE is, and is not

HIVE shows Honeybee's clients the status of their books, the questions and
document requests that need them, and what happens next, and lets
Honeybee's staff review and approve that work. Access is by invitation:
your Honeybee team sets up your account, and the app cannot create one on
its own. HIVE is not a ledger and holds no balances, amounts, or account
numbers; your accounting system and your permanent records stay where they
are.

### 3. The information HIVE handles

- **Your account**: the email address you sign in with, an account
  identifier, and the memberships Honeybee assigns to you, which decide
  which client and business you can see. Staff accounts also hold an
  authenticator enrollment used as a second sign-in step.
- **What you provide through the app**: the documents you add, the
  answers you write to Honeybee's questions, and the notes and decisions
  Honeybee's staff record while reviewing them.
- **Sign-in codes**: a one-time code is emailed to you each time you sign
  in.
- **Nothing else.** HIVE contains no advertising, analytics, or crash
  reporting software, uses no tracking identifier, and keeps nothing on
  your device except your signed-in session, stored in the device's secure
  storage until you sign out or your account is deleted.

### 4. Why we use it

To provide the service: to show you what applies to you and only that,
to move your requests and documents through Honeybee's review, and to
keep a record of who did what. Every sensitive action leaves an audit
receipt that names identifiers, never content. We do not sell information,
use it for advertising, or build profiles from it.

### 5. Who else sees it

Providers that act for Honeybee, under contract, and only to the extent
needed:

- **Supabase**, which hosts HIVE's database and sign-in service in the
  United States.
- **Resend**, which delivers the sign-in code emails and therefore sees
  your email address and the code, never your documents or answers.
- **Apple and Google**, which distribute the app through their stores and
  do not receive your content.

Beyond these, information is disclosed only when the law requires it.

### 6. How it is protected

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Access is enforced by the database
itself, per client and per business, so an account sees only what it was
granted; staff must complete a second sign-in step before they can see
client content; accounts exist by invitation only; and sensitive actions
are logged.

### 7. How long it is kept

Your session stays on your device until you sign out or your account is
deleted. Your account exists until you ask for its deletion (section 8).
Documents, answers, and review records belong to the business records
Honeybee keeps for the client you work with and are retained under
Honeybee's record-keeping policy: [retention periods, or a reference to
the published policy].

### 8. Your choices

- **Deleting your account.** In HIVE, open **Account** and choose
  **Request account deletion**; you can withdraw the request from the same
  place until Honeybee completes it. If you can no longer sign in, email
  info@myhbcfo.com. Deletion removes your access and the account you sign
  in with. Business records, including documents and answers you provided,
  are kept under the record-keeping policy above; where they name who
  acted, they keep an internal reference and not your name or email once
  the account is gone. Honeybee confirms the deletion by email.
- **Seeing or correcting your information.** Email info@myhbcfo.com.
